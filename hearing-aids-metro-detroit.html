Start with your listening needs, not a brand name

Two people can have the same "hearing loss" but need very different solutions. A good starting point is a professional hearing evaluation and a conversation about your goals, like hearing speech in restaurants, hearing family voices clearly, or feeling less tired at the end of the day.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Where do you struggle the most: quiet rooms, restaurants, meetings, or the car?
  • Do you have tinnitus (ringing in the ears) or sound sensitivity?
  • Do you use the phone or video calls often?
  • Do you want rechargeable batteries?

Quick FAQs

Where should I go for hearing aids in Metro Detroit?

Start with a diagnostic hearing evaluation from a licensed audiologist who can match recommendations to your hearing test, listening needs, and budget. Hinderliter Hearing Services is located in Birmingham and serves patients from Oakland County and the surrounding Metro Detroit communities.

What is the difference between an audiologist and a hearing aid dealer?

Audiologists are licensed hearing healthcare professionals who diagnose hearing and balance conditions and provide hearing aid care. Hearing aid dealers focus on fitting hearing aids and may not provide the same level of clinical evaluation and medical referral guidance.

Do I need a hearing test before buying hearing aids?

Yes. A recent hearing test helps confirm the type and degree of hearing loss and guides which features matter most for your ears and your lifestyle.

How many follow-up visits should I expect?

Most people need multiple adjustments at first. Ask any provider what the first 60 to 90 days looks like and whether follow-ups are included.

Does Medicare cover hearing aids?

No. Medicare states that it does not cover hearing aids or exams for fitting hearing aids (Medicare.gov).
