Tinnitus treatment in Southfield, MI

If you are hearing ringing, buzzing, hissing, or other phantom sounds, you are not alone. Tinnitus is common, and it can feel stressful and distracting. The right first step is an evaluation that looks at your hearing and your symptoms, so you can get a clear plan for what to do next.

What we do for tinnitus patients

At Hinderliter Hearing Services, tinnitus care typically starts with a diagnostic hearing evaluation. Many people with tinnitus also have hearing loss, and understanding your hearing profile helps guide next steps. Depending on your needs, tinnitus management may include education, sound therapy options, hearing aids (when appropriate), and strategies to reduce stress and improve sleep.

If you are searching for tinnitus treatment near Southfield, MI, call (248) 430-4416 to schedule an appointment at our Birmingham office.

Tinnitus: when to seek urgent medical care

  • Tinnitus after a head injury.
  • Tinnitus with sudden hearing loss, facial weakness, or a spinning sensation (vertigo).
  • Tinnitus that beats in time with your pulse (pulsatile tinnitus).

Educational information, not medical advice. If you think you may have an emergency, call 911.

What should I do first for ringing in the ears?

Start by scheduling a hearing test. Keep a short list of triggers (stress, sleep, caffeine, noise exposure) and how long symptoms last, so we can discuss patterns at your visit.

Can hearing aids help tinnitus?

For many people with both hearing loss and tinnitus, hearing aids can reduce tinnitus awareness by improving access to everyday sound. Your audiologist can explain whether hearing aids are likely to help based on your hearing test results and your goals.
